Privacy Policy

Express Delivery ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you use our tracking and delivery services.

What we collect. To create and track shipments we store sender and recipient names, contact details, addresses, and package details you provide. Administrators can also record delivery updates and notes.

How we use it. Solely to deliver, track, and support your shipments, and to send optional notifications you have consented to. We do not sell your personal data.

Public tracking. The public tracking page exposes only the information needed to follow a parcel. Sensitive recipient contact details and private administrative notes are never shown publicly.

Security. Access to administrative functions requires authentication, and passwords are stored hashed. All data is validated on the server.

This is a demonstration platform. Do not enter real sensitive personal data.

Terms of Service
